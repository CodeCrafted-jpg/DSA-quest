TRACK 04 Education AI-Powered Personalized Learning Ecosystem Can you identify a 
patient's potential deterioration before it becomes an emergency—without overwhelming healthcare 
professionals with false alarms? PROBLEM STATEMENT Build an intelligent learning platform that can determine what
 a student knows, what they misunderstand, why they are struggling, and what they should learn next. The system should 
 construct a continuously adapting learning pathway based on performance, learning speed, mistakes, engagement, and knowledge gaps. 
 The system should analyze answers—not merely whether they are correct, but how the student arrived at them. MINIMUM REQUIREMENTS Student 
 knowledge modelling Adaptive difficulty Personalized learning paths Automatic question generation Weak-topic detection Learning-progress prediction Teacher analytics dashboard
  HARD MODE Two students answering the same question incorrectly should potentially receive different explanations and learning paths depending on their underlying misconceptions. 
  CORE CHALLENGE Can you create an AI tutor that understands a student's misunderstanding rather than simply telling them the correct answer?
